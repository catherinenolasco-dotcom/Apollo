Case studies

People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

Ridecell had no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount tracked in spreadsheets, and no tracked channel for employee questions.

What I did

Built a 20+ procedure SOP library with owners and annual reviews, ASKPeople (Jira and Confluence with tiered SLAs), and Sapling as the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier.

Result

Grew from 3 to 5 countries, passed 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, cut approval cycles from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and resolved 5-10 tickets a day in 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process, which created real audit risk. Early feedback was that a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form that requires documentation and approval before any HRIS update, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows with an owner on every task.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

  • Claude
  • Notion
  • Rippling
  • Slack

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization affected 18 employees across the US and India, and leadership had no way to see total cost.

What I did

Built AI-enabled severance and cost modeling with Claude and Gemini that calculated severance, COBRA cost, and COBRA months owed, drafted agreements, and tracked every step on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

First-time visibility into reorganization cost, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Google Sheets
  • Rippling

A first six months

A proposed plan built around the responsibilities in the role description.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

  • Meet People, IT, Data, Payroll, Finance, and Legal partners and learn how each uses the systems.
  • Map current systems, integrations, and data flows, and collect requirements from the people who use them.
  • Run a light audit like the 60-day one I did at Prezent AI to find the biggest gaps.
Days 31 to 60

Design the first moves

  • Draft a multi-quarter People Systems roadmap with People leadership and IT.
  • Pick quick wins, using the Employee Action Form pattern of documented, approved changes.
  • Agree on how each initiative's impact will be measured.
Days 61 to 90

Ship and measure

  • Configure, test, and release the first improvements with change management and training.
  • Stand up reporting that shows what the investment delivered.
  • Pilot one practical AI workflow with a small team first.
By month 6

The outcome

  • A roadmap in delivery, with owners and a monthly planning rhythm.
  • Vendors reviewed against measurable value.
  • Cleaner People data, and AI and automation opportunities shipped.

This is a starting point. I would adjust it after the first conversations with People leadership, IT, and the partners who use these systems.
